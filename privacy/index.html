Coaching Codex is run by Digitalise Solutions. This page explains what we collect and why, in plain words.

Free Starter Pack sign-up

If you sign up, we keep your first name and email address so we can send you the Starter Pack and a short series of tips (5 emails over about a week, then occasional updates). Emails are sent through MailerLite, our email provider. You can unsubscribe with one click from any email, or email lawrence.lim@digitalisesolutions.com and we will delete your details.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your full card details. Stripe shares your email and order details with us so we can deliver your guide and handle refunds.

Interactive guides

Details you save in an interactive guide stay on your own device (browser storage) and are never sent to us. Use "Forget my details and saved prompts" to clear them. Prompts you paste into an AI assistant are handled by that assistant's provider under its own terms.
